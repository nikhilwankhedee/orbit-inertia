--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAN THE 1.81 s OFFSET BE EXPLAINED?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The raw timestamp data shows:
    1. A large epoch offset (~3600 s = ~1.0 hours)
    2. A drift (~313 s) matching the phone gap

  The synchronization process removed the large epoch offset
  and accounted for the gap. The remaining ~1.81 s offset
  is NOT visible in the raw timestamp data.

  The raw timestamps do not provide sufficient resolution
  to identify a ~1.81 s offset. The vehicle timestamp has
  0.1 s resolution, and the phone DATE has ~1 ms resolution.
  However, the synchronization process uses these timestamps
  to align the streams, and the residual offset persists.

  CONCLUSION:
  The dataset does not provide sufficient evidence to attribute
  the measured 1.81 s inter-stream offset to a specific
  timestamp epoch difference.

  The 1.81 s offset must be treated as an empirically
  calibrated inter-stream alignment parameter.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RECOMMENDATION FOR DR EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Treat the 1.81 s offset as an empirical alignment parameter:

  1. Do NOT claim it represents 'phone IMU latency'
  2. Do NOT claim it represents 'vehicle CAN bus delay'
  3. DO state: 'Phone Gyro Pitch and vehicle yaw rate are
     aligned with an empirical offset of +1.81 ± 0.06 s'
  4. For DR evaluation, shift the vehicle yaw rate signal
     backward by 1.81 s (or equivalently, shift the phone
     gyro pitch signal forward by 1.81 s)
  5. Report this as a calibrated parameter, not a physical
     measurement of sensor latency
